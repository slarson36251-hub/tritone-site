The Scott Larson Quartet is trombonist Scott Larson's own jazz group, and the lineup rotates from gig to gig. Scott calls on players from across the Bay Area scene, so each night has its own sound. Recent sets have featured Jay Sanders on piano, Eric Markowitz and Aaron Cohen on bass, and Smith Dobson and Ron Marabuto on drums. Each show page lists who's playing that night.
